About Splitaway

Welcome to Splitaway, the modern fintech approach to group expenses and trip management. We believe that traveling with friends should be about making memories, not doing awkward math.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to completely eliminate the friction of shared expenses. We saw the clunky spreadsheets, the confusing payback chains, and the outdated apps, and decided to build something better. A platform that is lightning fast, reliable, and beautifully designed.

Why We Built This

We've all been there — returning from an amazing group trip only to spend the next week trying to figure out who owes what. Splitaway is the solution. By combining real-time cloud sync, intelligent settlement algorithms, and an uncompromising dark-mode premium interface, we've created the tool we always wanted to use ourselves.
