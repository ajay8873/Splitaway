Premium Features

Splitaway is designed from the ground up to make group travel effortless.

💸 Smart Settlements Algorithm

Stop playing middleman. Our intelligent algorithm calculates the absolute minimum number of transactions required for everyone to be paid back. Instead of Alice paying Bob, Bob paying Charlie, and Charlie paying Dave, our system optimizes it so Alice simply pays Dave directly.

☁️ Instant Cloud Synchronization

Powered by Supabase, your trips are backed up the moment you enter them. Log in from your phone, your laptop, or your friend's tablet, and see your data instantly. No more lost spreadsheets.

📱 Offline Ready

Traveling without cell service? No problem. Splitaway stores your data locally first. You can add expenses while hiking or on an airplane, and they will securely sync to the cloud the next time you connect to the internet.

🎨 Dark Mode Aesthetics

We care about design. Splitaway features a custom, high-end "glassmorphism" aesthetic built on a dark-mode foundation, ensuring it looks incredible and is easy on your eyes in low-light environments.

🔒 Secure Authentication

Your data is protected. We utilize modern, passwordless-capable email authentication to ensure that only you have access to your dashboard and your expenses.
