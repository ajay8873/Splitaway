Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Splitaway. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and use our application.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect and process the following data about you:

  • Identity Data: Name, username, or similar identifier.
  • Contact Data: Email address.
  • Financial Data: We do not process direct payments. Financial data collected consists strictly of trip expense records entered manually by users.
  • Technical Data: Internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, and interaction data used solely for improving service performance.

3. How We Use Your Data

We only use your personal data to provide our core services, primarily including:

  • Registering you as a new user and securing your account.
  • Synchronizing your trip expense data across devices.
  • Managing our relationship with you, including notifying you of updates.

4. Data Security

We have implemented appropriate security measures, including modern encryption algorithms and secure database hosting (via Supabase), to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We will only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes we collected it for, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. You may delete your trips and account data at any time from within the application.

6. Your Legal Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, erasure, or restriction of processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Contact Us
